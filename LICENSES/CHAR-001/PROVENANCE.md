# Sendagaya Shino provenance

- Original character/model: **Sendagaya Shino**, pixiv Inc. / VRoid Project.
- Original license: **CC0 1.0**. The official VRoid FAQ says the model may be
  edited and used freely and that pixiv Inc. waived copyright and neighboring
  rights to the extent permitted by law.
- VRM 1.0 conversion: **Coatie (Koh-Tee)**, published on VRoid Hub.
- Hub conditions verified on 2026-09-21: avatar, corporate and individual
  commercial use, redistribution, alterations and redistribution of alterations
  are allowed; attribution is not required.
- Exact download: pinned YUI repository commit
  `02368b1288c05ceecbbe782832788250304a73a4`, whose adjacent provenance file
  identifies the same official source and converter.
- The SHA-256 digest is recorded in `SHA256.txt` and must match before import.

The courtesy credit is preserved in the project despite attribution not being
required.
